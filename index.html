Welcome to DWD Dance

A dance school built around the child, not the timetable

Donelon Wild School of Dance has been teaching young dancers across Stretford and the surrounding areas of Trafford since 2016 — and in that time one thing has never changed: every child is taught as an individual.

Some of our students arrive at three years old, wobbling through their first plié, and are still dancing with us as teenagers. Others join at eleven, having never danced before. Both are equally welcome. We teach ballet, tap and modern from complete beginner right through to student grade levels, and we prepare dancers for National Association of Teachers of Dancing (NATD) examinations when they’re ready — never before.

What parents tell us they notice first isn’t the technique. It’s the confidence.

What we teach

Three disciplines, one strong foundation

Most of our students take two or three styles — they complement each other beautifully. You’re very welcome to start with just one.

Ballet

The foundation of everything. Posture, poise, strength and control, taught with care and a good deal of imagination for the youngest dancers.

Tap

Accreditation

NATD examinations — progress you can actually see

We prepare our dancers for examinations with the National Association of Teachers of Dancing, one of the UK’s long-established examination boards for ballet, tap and modern.

Exams are entirely optional, and no child is ever entered before they’re ready. But for those who take them, the effect is remarkable: a clear goal, a syllabus that builds properly year on year, a certificate with their name on it — and, quietly, the discovery that hard work pays off.

  • Nationally recognised grades in ballet, tap and modern
  • Structured syllabus from pre-primary to student grade levels
  • Examination sessions held locally, with familiar faces around
  • Optional throughout — dance for the love of it if you prefer

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website stores on your device. Some are needed for a site to work at all; others help the site owner understand how the site is being used. This site uses as few as we can manage.

Cookies we use

This site does not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking cookies. The only thing it stores is your answer to the cookie notice.

NameTypePurposeExpires
dw-cookie-choice Essential Remembers whether you accepted or declined optional cookies, so we stop asking. Stored in your browser’s local storage rather than sent to us. Until you clear your browser data

Managing your choice

When you first visit, a notice at the bottom of the screen lets you accept all cookies or essential ones only. To change your mind, clear this site’s data in your browser settings and the notice will appear again.

You can also block or delete cookies entirely in your browser. The essential parts of this site will still work, but your cookie preference will be forgotten each visit.

Typefaces

Our pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser fetches the font files from Google’s servers, which tells Google your IP address. No cookie is set by this, and we receive nothing from it.

Our school

The school Bethany and Sally built

Donelon Wild School of Dance was founded in Stretford in 2016 by Bethany Donelon and Sally Wild — two dance teachers who put their own names above the door and have taught here ever since.

Our story

Built on teaching, not on numbers

Donelon Wild School of Dance was founded in 2016 by Bethany Donelon and Sally Wild. The name is simply theirs, which tells you most of what you need to know about how personally they take it. What began as a handful of classes has grown into a school running across four venues — Stretford, Flixton, Urmston and Davyhulme — and yet it has stayed, deliberately, a small school in feel.

We could have grown faster. We chose instead to keep classes small enough that every teacher knows every child by name, knows which one needs encouraging and which one needs slowing down, and notices when something isn’t right.

The result is a school where children stay. Dancers who joined us in our first year are still with us a decade later, and it is not unusual for a parent to tell us that dance class has been the one fixed point in a very busy childhood.

Teaching philosophy

How we teach — and why

Four principles that shape every class we run.

Technique taught properly, from the start

It is far easier to teach a good habit than to unpick a bad one. Even in our Pre-School classes, the positions are correct — simply introduced through play. Dancers who go on to higher grades find the foundation is already there.

Encouragement before correction

Children learn faster when they feel safe being wrong. Our teachers correct constantly, but they praise first — and no child is ever singled out or embarrassed in front of the class.

Progress at the child’s pace

Some dancers race through grades; others take two years over one. Both are fine. We enter children for examinations when they are ready, and we say so honestly when they’re not.

Dance as a life skill, not just a hobby

Only a few of our students will dance professionally. All of them leave with better posture, better discipline, better confidence in front of a room — and a memory of standing on a stage and being applauded.

Accreditation

NATD examination training

We are a National Association of Teachers of Dancing school. The NATD is one of the UK’s established examination boards for ballet, tap and modern, and its graded syllabus underpins how we structure our teaching from pre-primary through to student grade levels.

Working to a syllabus matters more than it might sound. It means each year builds properly on the last, that a child moving up a grade has genuinely earned it, and that the certificate at the end is recognised well beyond our own studio.

Examinations are optional throughout. Where a family prefers their child simply to dance for the pleasure of it, that is entirely welcome — and the teaching they receive is exactly the same.

Performance

Opportunities to perform — for everyone, not just the best

There is no audition to be in our show. If a dancer wants to be on that stage, we find them a place on it.

Our show, every other year

A full production in a proper theatre, with costumes, lighting and a curtain call. Staged every other year, with rehearsals building through the months before — and the whole school takes part, from three-year-olds to seniors.

Watch weeks & demonstrations

Termly open classes where families come in to see the work in progress. They run every year, including the years between shows, and are often where the youngest dancers first realise people are watching — and rather like it.

Festivals & community events

Local performances and festival opportunities for dancers who want the extra challenge, with preparation and coaching from their teachers.

Your peace of mind

A safe and supportive environment

You are leaving your child with us. We take that seriously, and we’re happy to answer any question you have about how we operate.

Checked, trained teachers

All teaching staff hold an enhanced DBS check and current first-aid training, and our safeguarding policy is available to any parent on request.

Clear drop-off and collection

Children are signed in and out of every class, and released only to a named adult. Please let us know in advance if someone different is collecting.

Kindness is non-negotiable

We are firm about behaviour towards one another. Every child should be able to walk into class knowing they will be treated well — by their teachers and by the other dancers.

Safe, age-appropriate training

Pointe work, flexibility work and advanced technique are introduced only when a dancer’s body is ready. We will always say no to a request that isn’t safe yet.

Open communication

If something is worrying you — a friendship, a grade, a fee — tell us early. We would far rather have a five-minute conversation than lose a dancer quietly.

Everyone belongs here

Every child is welcome at DW Dance regardless of background, body shape, ability or experience. We teach the dancer in front of us.

Our school

About us

Donelon Wild School of Dance was founded in Stretford in 2016 by Bethany Donelon and Sally Wild, and they still teach here every week.

Our story

A small school, on purpose

We started in 2016 with a handful of classes in Stretford, teaching ballet, tap and modern to children aged 3 to 18.

We have kept classes small so that every teacher knows every child by name, and we prepare dancers for NATD examinations when they are ready.

Dancers who joined us in our first year are still with us today, which is the part we are proudest of.

Teaching philosophy

How we teach — and why

Four things that shape every class we run.

Taught properly from day one

Even in our Beginners classes the positions are correct — just introduced through play.

Encouragement before correction

We correct constantly, but we praise first, and no child is singled out in front of the class.

Progress at the child’s pace

Some dancers race through grades, others take longer. Both are fine, and we enter children for examinations only when they are ready.

Dance as a life skill

Whatever a dancer goes on to do, they leave us with better posture, more discipline and a lot more confidence.

On stage

Opportunities to perform

There is no audition to be in our show. If a dancer wants to be on that stage, we find them a place on it.

Our own show, every other year

We put on our own show every other year in a proper theatre, with costumes, lighting and a curtain call. The whole school takes part, from the youngest dancers to the seniors.

Local festival work

We also enter local dance festivals. Festival work is for selected dancers who have reached the required grade, and their teacher will let you know when the time is right.

A safe and supportive environment

You are leaving your child with us. We take that seriously, and we’re happy to answer any question you have about how we operate.

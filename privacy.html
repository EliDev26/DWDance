Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Donelon Wild School of Dance (“we”, “us”) is a dance school based in Stretford, Greater Manchester. For data protection purposes we are the data controller for the information described below. You can contact us at miss.bethany@dwdance.co.uk or on 07910 603360.

The information we collect

  • Enquiry details: the name, email address and subject you give us on the contact form, and anything you tell us if you email or telephone us directly.
  • Registration details: where a child joins the school, we also hold contact and emergency contact details, relevant medical or additional-needs information you choose to share, and a record of classes attended and examinations taken.
  • Photographs and video: only where you have given specific consent, for example for our show programme or social media.
  • Website usage: nothing. This website has no analytics or tracking — see our cookies policy.

Why we use it, and our lawful basis

  • To answer your enquiry and arrange your child’s first class — our legitimate interest in responding to you.
  • To run classes, take payment and administer examinations — performance of our agreement with you.
  • To keep children safe, including emergency contact and medical information — our legitimate interest in safeguarding, and your consent for health information.
  • To send occasional school news, timetable changes and show information — our legitimate interest in keeping families informed. You can opt out at any time.
  • To publish photographs or video — only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information and we do not pass it to third parties for marketing. We share only what is necessary with:

  • the National Association of Teachers of Dancing, where a child is entered for an examination;
  • venue operators, where a booking requires it;
  • our email and payment providers, who process data on our behalf. The contact form does not send your details to any other company — it opens a message in your own email app, addressed to us;
  • emergency services or safeguarding authorities, where a child’s welfare requires it.

Our dancewear store is operated by a separate company at ddw.mydancestore.co.uk. Anything you enter there is governed by that company’s own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to a child joining the school are deleted after 12 months. Student records are kept for as long as a child dances with us and for [X] years afterwards, so that we can confirm examination results and respond to any later query.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you or your child, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw any consent you have given. Write to miss.bethany@dwdance.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Children’s information

Because we teach children, most of the information we hold is provided by a parent or guardian on their behalf, and we deal with parents and guardians on all data protection matters for children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page whenever our practices change, and will note the date of the most recent revision at the top.

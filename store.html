FAQs

Common questions

There’s no rush. Comfortable clothes your child can move in are fine for a first class — buy once they have settled and you know which classes they are taking.

Dancewear sizes run smaller than high-street clothing, so use the size guide on the product page rather than your child’s usual size. Shoes matter most: they need to fit properly to be safe, so ask your child’s teacher before you order if you are unsure.

Both are handled by our dancewear partner, with current details shown at checkout on ddw.mydancestore.co.uk.
